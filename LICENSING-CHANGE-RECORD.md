# ROBOTRAITS — Licensing Change and Due-Diligence Record

Project owner: Aaron Bailot (PITN)
Repository: https://github.com/PITN374/ROBOTRAITS

## Purpose

This record documents the project owner's decision to clarify
the intended licensing position for original ROBOTRAITS materials
and establish a commercial licensing policy.

## Background

The project owner identified an unintended MIT licensing designation
shortly after the repository's public release.

The project owner is documenting the issue and publishing explicit
copyright and commercial licensing notices to clarify the intended
licensing model for original materials owned or controlled by PITN.

## Corrective Measures

- Publish a dedicated copyright notice.
- Publish a commercial licensing policy.
- Preserve the repository's existing commit history.
- Review and correct inconsistent license references where appropriate.
- Maintain a record of subsequent licensing changes.

## Publication and Access

The repository remains publicly accessible.

No claim is made here that no person accessed, cloned, forked,
or copied the repository. Any statement about downloads or access
must be supported by available records.

## Scope

This record documents the owner's stated intent and corrective
actions. It does not retroactively cancel an existing license
grant or alter third-party rights.

## Ownership

The project owner asserts ownership of original ROBOTRAITS materials
created and owned by Aaron Bailot. Materials subject to third-party
rights must be treated according to their applicable terms.

## Record Integrity

Subsequent changes should be recorded in Git commit history.
This document should not be backdated.
