1. Purpose

This document establishes the provenance and development-record framework for ROBOTRAITS, a trait-based robotic intelligence ontology designed to represent computational behavioral traits, agent profiles, behavioral policies, operational context, and adaptation history.

It supports the identification, documentation, and preservation of original project materials associated with PITN.ai, ROBOTRAITS, and ROBOAGENTIC.

2. Project Origin and Attribution

PITN.ai records ROBOTRAITS as a project initiative for representing computational behavioral traits in robotic and autonomous intelligent agents.

PITN.ai asserts originating authorship of the ROBOTRAITS concept and original project materials identified as PITN-created in the project's source-control, design, and provenance records.

4. Source-Control Evidence

The project preserves source-control evidence to document the evolution of ROBOTRAITS.

Relevant evidence includes:

Repository URL and repository ownership.
Commit identifiers and recorded timestamps.
Author and contributor metadata.
Pull requests and reviewed changes, where applicable.
Version tags and release records.
Archived source files and historical documents.
SHA-256 checksums of published artifacts.

Existing history is preserved without backdating or rewriting commits to imply an earlier development date.

7. Third-Party Sources and Contributions

Every third-party ontology, vocabulary, software dependency, dataset, diagram, or substantial contribution must be recorded with its source, version, license, applicable terms, and intended use.

External research may inform terminology or semantic mappings. Its underlying concepts and source materials must not be represented as exclusively owned by ROBOTRAITS merely because they are referenced or integrated.

Contributors must identify material authored independently and material obtained from third parties.

8. PITN.ai and ROBOPARTS Integration

ROBOTRAITS represents behavioral configuration and semantic descriptions of computational traits.

ROBOPARTS represents component, machine, assembly, and lifecycle relationships.

The integration record links these domains through explicit identifiers while keeping physical component identity, behavioral profiles, verified capabilities, and operational authorization distinct.
