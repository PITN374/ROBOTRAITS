2. Scope of the ROBOTRAITS initiative

ROBOTRAITS defines a proposed semantic framework for representing computational behavioral traits in robotic and autonomous agents, including:

Behavioral trait dimensions and operational definitions.
Trait values, scales, profiles, confidence, and provenance.
Behavioral policies and cognitive processes.
Communication, cooperation, exploration, learning, and adaptation.
Operational contexts, capabilities, and roles.
Agent identity, safety constraints, and authorized adaptation.
Versioned semantic identifiers and validation rules.

ROBOTRAITS is intended to complement PITN.ai and ROBOPARTS infrastructure. It distinguishes behavioral configuration from physical component identity, verified capability, and operational authorization.
